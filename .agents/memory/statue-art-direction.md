All statue portraits should match the user's November 14, 2026 reference screenshots: detailed white-marble busts, centered consistently on black square backgrounds. Use this as the collection-wide direction, not the older pixel-art look. Keep portraits at 768×768 and optimize them for the offline app bundle. Identify generated or reconstructed likenesses as uncertain rather than authenticated portraits.

**Why:** Resizing the earlier pixel-style portraits made them the correct dimensions but did not match the detailed marble busts the user showed in the app. The collection must remain usable offline, and generated art should not imply a historically verified likeness.

**How to apply:** Use this reference for every new or replaced statue; preserve the bust crop, marble tone, black background, 768×768 size, names, and quote data, and label AI reconstructions clearly.
