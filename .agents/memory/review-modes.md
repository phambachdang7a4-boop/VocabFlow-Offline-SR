---
name: Review modes
description: Product behavior and limits for Basic, Speed, and Timebound review sessions.
---

Basic review defaults to a maximum of 100 words per session; learners may choose 50, 100, 200, or 400. The chosen cap applies to regular vocabulary and idiom review queues. Speed Mode skips answer details and advances only after the review result is saved. Timebound is 10 minutes of active app time and ends with a partial summary when time runs out.

**Why:** The learner asked for a shorter standard session, a faster feedback-free mode, and a timer that does not punish time spent with the app in the background.

**How to apply:** Preserve spaced-repetition writes and review statistics in every mode. Reuse the app's active-time clock for Timebound so background time does not count.
