---
name: Review modes
description: Product behavior and limits for Basic, Speed, and Timebound review sessions.
---

Basic review defaults to a maximum of 100 words per session; learners may choose 50, 100, 200, or 400. The chosen cap applies to regular vocabulary and idiom review queues. Speed Mode skips answer details and advances only after the review result is saved. Timebound defaults to 10 minutes, with offline settings for 5, 10, 15, or 20 minutes; the chosen duration is captured at session start and ends with a partial summary when active time runs out.

**Why:** The learner asked for a shorter standard session, a faster feedback-free mode, and a timer that does not punish time spent with the app in the background. They later approved selectable Timebound durations while keeping 10 minutes as the default.

**How to apply:** Preserve spaced-repetition writes and review statistics in every mode. Reuse the app's active-time clock for Timebound so background time does not count; apply a changed duration only to sessions started afterward.
