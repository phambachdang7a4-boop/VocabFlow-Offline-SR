---
name: VocabFlow performance priority
description: Product tradeoff for app size versus interaction smoothness.
---

VocabFlow may be larger when needed; prioritize a smooth, responsive app over minimizing APK size.

**Why:** The owner explicitly prefers smooth performance even when it increases app size.

**How to apply:** When choosing between a smaller APK and faster startup, less lag, or responsive screens, favor smooth performance. Measure on Android and avoid claiming a target is met until device data confirms it.
